Who is Rafael Nunes?

Rafael Nunes dos Santos is a full stack developer specialized in .NET, based in Paulínia, Brazil. With a postgraduate degree in .NET Systems Architecture from FIAP, he builds web systems, APIs and integrations, with experience in logistics (TMS and EDI) and ERPs, as well as Flutter and React Native apps, Angular websites and Unity games. He uses artificial intelligence in everyday development, with technical review and a focus on quality.
