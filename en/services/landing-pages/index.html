What is a landing page?

A landing page is a page built for a single goal — generating a lead, a quote request or a sale. Unlike a website with many loose sections, it guides visitors through a clear sequence: problem, solution, proof and call to action, while loading fast and ranking well on Google.

Who it is for

  • Service providers who rely on referrals and want to show up on Google
  • Companies launching a new product or service
  • Businesses with an old, slow website that breaks on mobile
  • Professionals who want their own portfolio

Problems it solves

  • A website that does not show up in search
  • A slow page that loses visitors before it loads
  • Visits that never turn into leads or quotes
  • Relying only on social media to be found

What can be built

  • Responsive layout for phone, tablet and desktop
  • Prerendered pages optimized for speed
  • Technical SEO: metadata, structured data and sitemap
  • WhatsApp buttons with prefilled messages
  • Quote forms connected to your email or CRM
  • Google Analytics and conversion tracking

How it works

  1. 1

    Briefing. I learn the audience, the page goal and what sets your service apart.

  2. 2

    Structure and copy. I lay out the section flow and help write clear, direct copy.

  3. 3

    Design and development. I build a responsive, fast page with technical SEO.

  4. 4

    Review. You review it live on a staging address before release.

  5. 5

    Launch. Domain, hosting, Analytics and Search Console set up.

What you get

  • Page live on your domain
  • Responsive layout
  • Technical SEO set up
  • WhatsApp and form integration
  • Analytics and Search Console
  • Full access to the files
  • Angular
  • TypeScript
  • SCSS
  • SEO
  • Google Analytics

How much does it cost?

A landing page has a tighter scope: the price depends on the number of sections, whether copy and images are ready, and integrations such as forms and a blog. You get a fixed written quote before we start.

How long does it take?

With copy and images defined, a landing page usually goes live in a few days to two weeks.
