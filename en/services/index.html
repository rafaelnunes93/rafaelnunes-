Full stack development with .NET and Angular

Complete web systems with a solid C# back end and a modern Angular interface.

Full stack development means building every layer of a web system: the database, business rules and APIs on the back end, and the screens users see on the front end. In my case that means C# and .NET with SQL Server on the back end and Angular on the front end, with one person accountable for the whole solution.

See details, timeline and pricing

REST APIs and system integration

Your systems, partners and suppliers exchanging data automatically, with no manual typing.

A system integration is an automatic bridge that lets two or more software products exchange information — via REST APIs, webhooks, files or standards such as EDI. Instead of someone copying data from one screen to another, the system itself sends, receives, validates and logs every exchange.

See details, timeline and pricing

End-to-end SaaS: from idea to operation

From MVP to product at scale: architecture, billing, multi-tenancy and operations.

SaaS (software as a service) is a system accessed over the internet and sold by subscription, where many customer companies use the same application with separated data (multi-tenant). Building a SaaS involves, beyond the screens, account sign-up, plans, recurring billing, security and an architecture that can grow.

See details, timeline and pricing

Mobile apps with Flutter or React Native

One codebase for Android and iOS, connected to your APIs with native performance.

A cross-platform app is written once and published to both Android and iOS. With Flutter or React Native, the app performs close to native and looks consistent on both platforms, which lowers development and maintenance costs compared with two separate apps.

See details, timeline and pricing

Landing pages and business websites

Fast, responsive pages designed to turn visits into leads.

A landing page is a page built for a single goal — generating a lead, a quote request or a sale. Unlike a website with many loose sections, it guides visitors through a clear sequence: problem, solution, proof and call to action, while loading fast and ranking well on Google.

See details, timeline and pricing

Game development with Unity

2D and 3D games with mechanics, UI and multi-platform publishing.

Building a game with Unity involves programming gameplay mechanics, physics, enemy AI, inventory and UI in C#, as well as integrating art and sound and publishing to PC, web or mobile. Unity is one of the most widely used engines in the world, which makes it easier to find resources and port the game to other platforms.
